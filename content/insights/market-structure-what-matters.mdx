Modern markets are a network: multiple venues, multiple rulebooks, and multiple incentives. The goal isn’t to memorize every detail—it’s to understand the forces that shape outcomes.

## Liquidity is conditional

Displayed liquidity is not a promise. It’s an invitation that can change with microstructure dynamics, volatility, and queue position.

## Fragmentation is a design constraint

When prices emerge across venues, your systems must reason about:

- stale signals
- routing latency
- partial fills

## Measurement beats intuition

The fastest way to improve outcomes is to instrument and analyze. Data turns “we think” into “we know.”
