Observability is how you keep promises under stress. It’s the difference between “we’re down” and “this service is dropping 3% of writes due to a regression in a single region.”

## Prefer wide over deep

Start by answering “what is broken” quickly across the entire system. Then drill into root cause.

## Make unknown states explicit

If dashboards only show green and red, you’ll miss yellow—the slow failures that become outages.

## Incident review is a systems design practice

Postmortems aren’t about blame. They’re about improving the system that produced the outcome.
