Resiliency is not a single feature—it’s a posture. In execution systems, that posture is expressed through explicit trade-offs: what you optimize for, what you degrade first, and what you refuse to compromise.

## Start with a latency budget

Latency budgets are constraints, not goals. A budget forces clarity:

- Where do you spend time?
- What can you cache?
- What can fail safely?

## Treat failure domains as product boundaries

If one dependency can take you down, it isn’t a dependency—it’s a single point of failure you haven’t admitted yet.

## Operational hygiene is a user experience

Incident reviews, dashboards, and alerts aren’t “internal tooling.” They are the interface between reality and your system.
